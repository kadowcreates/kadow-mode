# Calibration log format

Keep your calibration log outside this folder, next to your audit outputs: one row per audit, added after the report is final (`METHOD.md` § Calibration). Copy the table below to start it. Change a rating scale or the action priority table only when two or more rows point the same way.

| Date | Method commit | Scope | Reviewer | Risks | Impact changed at hold 2 | Unconfirmed impacts | Verifier overturned | Grades changed at hold 3 | Residual AP high · medium · low | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Changes made

Record each change to your copy of the method that calibration justified. Give the reason, never the rows or the system they came from.
